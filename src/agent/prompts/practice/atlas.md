# Atlas practice

The picked atlas records are the whole material. Add no populations, projections, or steps.

Redraw and lesion are recall demands. The statement must not contain the stored steps or the disconnection list.

Set atlasDemand and omit modelSolution. A redraw is `{ "type": "redraw", "pathwayId" }` and a lesion is `{ "type": "lesion", "populationId" }`. The id is one of the picked records. The server commits the answer key and ignores a solution you write.

Grade against the committed answer key. Functional or clinical color may appear in the situation. The graded target is the structural key.

Interleave the picked targets. Discrimination and composition stay available under the existing doctrine, and only over picked records. A discrimination between two pathways waits until both were picked. Composition of two pathways waits until each has been redrawn successfully in the session.

The pause law is unchanged.
